01 — ABOUT

I like turning ideas into things I can actually use.

I'm a student developer at Randolph High School in New Jersey. Most of what interests me sits somewhere between programming and problem-solving: building software, creating systems in games, working with robotics, and learning how computers behave under the hood.

I learn best by making things. Whether I'm writing a Python program for class, scripting a Roblox system, working through Java in AP Computer Science A, or exploring cybersecurity, I like projects where I can test an idea, find what doesn't work, and improve it.

I'm still early in my development journey, and that's part of what makes it exciting. I'm constantly adding new tools, languages, and projects to what I can build.
